A few useful answers

Know what comes next.

Do I need an account to use the workspace?

Yes. Create an account or log in to access your salon’s workspace. The homepage illustrations and scripted chat are examples; they do not create bookings or send messages.

Are payments and automated reminders live?

BeautyDesk records payments received outside the app. It does not collect deposits, charge subscriptions or process refunds. Confirmation and reminder previews remain unsent; live automated reminders are not enabled.

What does WhatsApp need before customer use?

Your salon’s provider connection must be configured and a real end-to-end conversation verified. Customers review the appointment details before explicitly confirming a booking.

Can I use photo estimates?

The workspace contains the photo catalogue and setup controls. Photo analysis is off. You can prepare the catalogue; activation needs a separate reviewed provider and privacy release and an authorised real WhatsApp test. Matching returns 1–3 text suggestions from saved services and prices, not generated hairstyle images. There is no browser photo upload.

Can I bring clients from another system?

A self-service import is not available. Supported imports, any migration assistance and costs need to be confirmed before you plan a move.

What needs to be confirmed before a paid launch?

PayFast activation and remaining commercial terms, reviewed privacy and legal information, support arrangements and production workflow testing. Account recovery and email verification are not yet available; do not assume a support response time or migration service has been promised.
